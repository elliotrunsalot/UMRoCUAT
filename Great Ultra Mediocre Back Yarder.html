Orbital Trajectory (The Course)

Clockwise orbit? Counter-clockwise orbit? In deep space, celestial mechanics govern all motion. Mission Control will determine the orbital vector direction prior to the first launch siren.

The trajectory consists of 6.71 km (4.167 miles) of undulating alpine fire trails, planetary dust, and mountain gradients. Each loop launches from the Base Corral at Blue Range Spaceport and exits via the northern airlock.

🛰️ Trajectory Vector Alpha: Clockwise Orbit

Ascend Blue Range Road towards Poo Bear Corner (scanners on for native wildlife). Climb north, then glide down the Pokey Crack chute and through native timber to Dead Horse Gate Road.

Bank right, ascend the heights of Burnt Knob, and marvel at the panoramic view of the cosmic expanse. Descend towards Pissing Contest Point, wind past Facebook Hill, and embark on a short hyperspace excursion down Gumbilical Lane.

Re-align with Facebook Hill, descend the rocky slope, and touch down back at the Blue Range Spaceport Corral. Mission nominal.

🛰️ Trajectory Vector Beta: Counter-Clockwise Orbit

Exit the north gate, banking hard right up the rocky slope to Facebook Hill. Veer right for your scouting mission down Gumbilical Lane.

Return to Facebook Hill and cruise down the wide logging highway. Weave around to Pissing Contest Point, keep left, scale Mt. GUMBY, and soak in the rugged Brindabella atmosphere.

Climb again to Burnt Knob before banking south. Slide through Pokey Crack, execute a sharp left at Poo Bear Corner, and descend smoothly to the Blue Range Spaceport Corral.

Directives of Engagement (Rules)

📜 Planetary Code of Conduct

  • The 60-Minute Law: Complete the 6.71 km loop within the hour. Be standing inside the starting corral when the launch siren sounds, or your mission is terminated (DNF).
  • No Hyperspace Pacing: No pacing or propulsion from non-competitors, dropped cosmonauts, crew members, or canine companions.
  • Cosmic Audio Sensor Protocol: Pay attention to the sounds of the surrounding wilderness. Fully enclosed headphones or noise-cancelling earbuds are strictly prohibited. Open-ear bone-conduction headsets are approved.
  • Zero Artificial Exo-Skeletons: Trekking poles and artificial walking sticks are banned. In the words of Mission Control: “Poles are for earthlings who fear gravity.”
  • Universal Respect: Treat fellow cosmonauts, volunteers, trees, trails, and planetary wildlife with absolute care.
  • Last Cosmonaut Standing: The GUMBY continues indefinitely until only one runner completes a solo loop. If no runner can finish a solo lap after all others drop, nobody wins.
  • GumBABY & GumbyMOON Sprints: Same yard format, but the final lap (Lap 8 for GumBABY, Lap 4 for GumbyMOON) is a hyperspace dash for victory. Everyone else who completes the distance is a classified Finisher.

Spaceport Life Support (HQ & Camping)

Blue Range Spaceport is an authentic, off-grid mountain outpost. There is no mains electrical power and no treated tap water supply. Planetary survival requires self-sufficiency.

Permanent composting toilets and supplementary mobile hygiene units are positioned at base. Our Zero Space Junk Policy applies: pack out everything you bring in. Do not pollute the planetary surface.

⛽ Hydration & Fuel

Potable drinking water and electrolyte fuel tanks are stationed at the corral start/finish area. Keep hydration flasks replenished before each launch.

🍳 Base Galley

Gas cookers and communal prep counters are available in the camp kitchen shelter to heat cosmonaut rations and brew hot recovery fluids.

🏪 Supply Depots

Civilisation and supermarket depots are 45 minutes away in Canberra. Arrive fully provisioned with your rations, warm layers, batteries, and spare gear.

🏕️ Spaceport Camping Logistics

Camping is $12 per cosmonaut per night. Please reserve your spot during online registration.

  • Friday Night (23 April): Open for all GUMBY and GumbyMOON runners and crew.
  • Saturday Night (24 April): GUMBY runners receive one crew member pass included free. GumBABY participants camping Saturday must be crewing a GUMBY cosmonaut.
  • Sunday Night (25 April): GUMBY runners receive one crew pass free. All other participants and crew welcome to camp for $12.
  • Strict Directive: No pets or companion animals permitted at the Spaceport. Leave Fido on Earth.

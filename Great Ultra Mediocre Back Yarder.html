The Mission Briefing

Ground Control to Mediocre Tom: For the 2027 edition, the Great Ultra Mediocre Back Yarder leaves Earth behind and ascends into the deep cosmos. Our forward exploration unit is establishing operations at the remote Blue Range Lunar Base in the rugged Brindabella Ranges for an interstellar weekend of gravitational pull, relentless loops, and cosmic camaraderie.

Whether you're braving the dark side of GumbyMOON, journeying for an entire sol to seek the mysterious GumBABY, surviving for three planetary revolutions in the GumbyGUMBO, or dodging cosmic GUMBY dust until you succumb to the safety of Quitter's Air-Lock, this mission will push you to your personal Event Horizon.

This deep-space expedition is organized and commanded by the cosmic directors at ULTRA Mediocre Runners of Canberra Incorporated.

The Missions

🌙 Twilight Recon // 4 Laps

The GumbyMOON

A four-lap twilight expedition taking flight on Friday evening, 23 April 2027. Covering 26.8 km under starlight, lunar shadows, and deep space silence. It is the ideal mission for dipping your space boots into the vast ocean of Last-One-Standing running. The first astronaut across the finish line on Lap 4 will be crowned the Star Child.

LAUNCH: Fri 23 Apr, 17:00 DISTANCE: 26.8 KM

The GUMBY (Backyard Ultra)

The main deep space mission launches Saturday morning, 24 April 2027. A brutal 6.71 km orbital loop executed every 60 minutes on the launch siren until only one cosmonaut remains standing. Every other participant will meet their inevitable Event Horizon (DNF), but fear not: our famous Quitters Air-Lock Ceremony awaits with an ice-cold Quitters Bitter and a collector's mission keepsake.

LAUNCH: Sat 24 Apr, 09:00 FORMAT: 1 Yard / Hour

Orbital Trajectory (The Course)

Clockwise orbit? Counter-clockwise orbit? In deep space, celestial mechanics govern all motion. Mission Control will determine the orbital vector direction prior to the first launch siren.

The trajectory consists of 6.71 km (4.167 miles) of undulating alpine fire trails, planetary dust, and mountain gradients. Each loop launches from the Base Corral at Blue Range Hut and exits via the northern airlock.

🛰️ Trajectory Vector Alpha: Clockwise Orbit

Ascend Blue Range Road towards Poo Bear Corner (scanners on for native wildlife). Climb north, then glide down the Pokey Crack chute and through native timber to Dead Horse Gate Road.

Bank right, ascend the heights of Mt. GUMBY, and marvel at the panoramic view of the cosmic expanse. Descend towards Pissing Contest Point, wind past Facebook Hill, and embark on a short hyperspace excursion down Gumbilical Lane.

Re-align with Facebook Hill, descend the rocky slope, and touch down back at the Base Corral. Mission nominal.

🛰️ Trajectory Vector Beta: Counter-Clockwise Orbit

Exit the north gate, banking hard right up the rocky slope to Facebook Hill. Veer right for your scouting mission down Gumbilical Lane.

Return to Facebook Hill and cruise down the wide logging highway. Weave around to Pissing Contest Point, keep left, scale Mt. GUMBY, and soak in the rugged Brindabella atmosphere.

Climb again, bank south, slide through Pokey Crack, execute a sharp left at Poo Bear Corner, and descend smoothly to the Base Corral.

Directives of Engagement (Rules)

📜 Planetary Code of Conduct

  • The 60-Minute Law: Complete the 6.71 km loop within the hour. Be standing inside the starting corral when the launch siren sounds, or your mission is terminated (DNF).
  • No Hyperspace Pacing: No pacing or propulsion from non-competitors, dropped astronauts, crew members, or canine companions.
  • Cosmic Audio Sensor Protocol: Pay attention to the sounds of the surrounding wilderness. Fully enclosed headphones or noise-cancelling earbuds are strictly prohibited. Open-ear bone-conduction headsets are approved.
  • Zero Artificial Exo-Skeletons: Trekking poles and artificial walking sticks are banned. In the words of Mission Control: “Poles are for earthlings who fear gravity.”
  • Universal Respect: Treat fellow cosmonauts, volunteers, trees, trails, and planetary wildlife with absolute care.
  • Last Astronaut Standing: The GUMBY continues indefinitely until only one runner completes a solo loop. If no runner can finish a solo lap after all others drop, nobody wins.
  • GumBABY & GumbyMOON Sprints: Same yard format, but the final lap (Lap 8 for GumBABY, Lap 4 for GumbyMOON) is an unrestricted race for victory. Everyone else who completes the distance is a classified Finisher.

Lunar Base Camp (HQ & Life Support)

Blue Range Hut is an authentic, off-grid mountain outpost. There is no mains electrical power and no treated tap water supply. Planetary survival requires self-sufficiency.

Permanent composting toilets and supplementary mobile hygiene units are positioned at base. Our Zero Space Junk Policy applies: pack out everything you bring in. Do not pollute the planetary surface.

⛽ Hydration & Fuel

Potable drinking water and Tailwind Endurance Fuel tanks are stationed at the corral start/finish area. Keep hydration flasks replenished before each launch.

🍳 Base Galley

Gas cookers and communal prep counters are available in the camp kitchen shelter to heat astronaut rations and brew hot recovery fluids.

🏪 Supply Depots

Civilization and supermarket depots are 45 minutes away in Canberra. Arrive fully provisioned with your rations, warm layers, batteries, and spare gear.

🏕️ Spaceport Camping Logistics

Camping is $12 per cosmonaut per night. Please reserve your spot during online registration.

  • Friday Night (23 April): Open for all GUMBY and GumbyMOON runners and crew.
  • Saturday Night (24 April): GUMBY runners receive one crew member pass included free. GumBABY participants camping Saturday must be crewing a GUMBY astronaut.
  • Sunday Night (25 April): GUMBY runners receive one crew pass free. All other participants and crew welcome to camp for $12.
  • Strict Directive: No pets or companion animals permitted at the lunar base. Leave Fido on Earth.

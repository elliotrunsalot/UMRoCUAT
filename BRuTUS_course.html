1. THE BRuTUS — 60KM COURSE MAP

Starting and finishing at historic Blue Range Hut, the 60km BRuTUS plunges into the Cotter Catchment past Vanitys Crossing to CP1, climbs over rolling hills to CP2 (Curries Road), storms into NSW up the knife-like peak of Mt. Coree (1,414m), regroups at CP3 (Coree Campground), grinds along Two Sticks & Blue Range Rd to CP4 (Knife Point), and survives the rutted Slippery Dip home.

Distance60.2 km
Elev Gain+2,000 m
Time Limit11 Hours
Peak (Coree)1,414 m
ACT II • THE GREAT BETRAYAL “3:45 AM Foot Washing • 4:00 AM Corin Dam Start • 4 Mystery Objects”

2. THE BTRAIL — 100KM COURSE MAP

Point-to-point from Corin Dam to Blue Range Hut. Climb 600m up Stockyard Spur in the first 2km and traverse the Brindabella spine via Mt. Ginini, Frankie CP1 (Mt. Franklin), Bendora Arboretum WP1, Mt. Aggie, Bulls Head CP2, Warks WP2, Marshalls Break, Wombat Creek CP3, Mt. Coree, Coreezy CP4, and Knife Point CP5. Collect all 4 summit mystery objects or your finish place is unratified!

Distance100.9 km
Elev Gain+3,000 m
Time Limit20 Hours
Peak (Ginini)1,752 m
ACT III • ET TU, BRUTE? “9:00 AM Start • The Lung Piercer & The Slippery Dip”

3. THE KNIFE — 14KM COURSE MAP

A concentrated 14.2km dagger of wild radiata pine, blackberry, and 680m of vertical betrayal from Blue Range Hut. Make a turn for the worst at Poo Bear Corner, suffer up the Lung Piercer to Knife Point Aid Station (CP1), and plunge down Genges Trail & the Slippery Dip before the deceptive twist on Dead Horse Gate Road.

Distance14.2 km
Elev Gain+680 m
Time Limit4 Hours
Knife Point1,241 m
